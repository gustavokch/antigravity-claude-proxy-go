Important routing facts (2026-10-11):
- Upstream `FetchAvailableModels` now defaults to Claude 5.5 tiers:
  `claude-opus-5-5-{low,medium,high}` and `claude-sonnet-5-5-{low,medium,high}`.
  Claude 5.5 models support 1,000,000 max context tokens and 128,000 max output tokens.
  Upstream represents thinking levels as integers: 1 (Low), 2 (Medium), 3 (High).
- Claude 4.6 is deprecated upstream: accounts migrated to 1.3.3 receive
  "Claude Sonnet 4.6 is no longer available. Please switch to Claude Sonnet 5.5."
  when requesting legacy IDs. The proxy must route legacy 4.6 model requests to
  Claude 5.5 equivalents.
- In upstream `agentModelSorts`, `gemini-pro-agent` replaces `gemini-3.1-pro-high`
  (documented in `deprecatedModelIds` mapping `gemini-3.1-pro-high` -> `gemini-pro-agent`).
  Both IDs must be supported.
- The upstream catalog is ACCOUNT-DEPENDENT. Accounts that still publish legacy
  models must continue resolving them via direct ID/display lookup and the
  account's own routing aliases before superseded routes are consulted.
- `gpt-oss-120b-medium` carries deprecation notice: scheduled for removal on
  November 2, 2026.
- (2026-10-16) Gemini 4 Argon family added (gemini-4-argon-medium, gemini-4-argon-high, gemini-4-argon-low):
  agy 1.3.3 defaults to Gemini 4 Argon (Medium). Context window is 1,048,576 tokens;
  max output tokens is 65,536 tokens. Upstream thinkingLevel: 1 (Low), 2 (Medium), 3 (High).
  FetchAvailableModels does not list Argon; the proxy synthesizes the tiers from Gemini 3.8 Flash (see applyGemini4Argon).
