Implemented boundary

starts on the first retryable failure or blocked selection. It accounts for
all retry waits and pre-event retries, records applied 429 cooldowns before
waiting, preserves the upstream cause and applied eligibility, and stops its
timer before forwarding the first upstream event. Healthy first attempts and
established streams remain uncapped by this window. HTTP/SSE completion and
write failures are propagated. OpenAI errors remain visible; the [DONE]
sentinel is unchanged because the consumer regression observes the error.
