Findings
--------
- The start burst (loadCodeAssist ... cascadeNuxes) is sent once per process,
  never again for a new conversation or a follow-up prompt.
- retrieveUserQuotaSummary is sent beside a model call or a conversation start
  only when the last one is at least about 60 s old (skipped at 59.7 s and
  59.8 s, sent at 67.8 s and later).
- fetchUserInfo, fetchAvailableModels and loadCodeAssist are sent together at
  a conversation start when the last fetchUserInfo is at least about 5 min old
  (skipped at 258.5 s, sent at 308.4 s).
- writeTrajectoryAcls and the title checkpoint appear at most once per
  conversation; agy does not send both for every conversation.
